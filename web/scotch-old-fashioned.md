---
title: "Scotch Old Fashioned"
template: recipe.mustache
slug: scotch-old-fashioned
time: "2 minutes"
makes: "1 cocktail"
cocktail: |
  # Scotch Old Fashioned
  glass: old-fashioned
  ice: large-cube
  method: build, stir

  spine:
    2    scotch
    0.5  maple-syrup

  accent:
    2 dashes chocolate-bitters
ingredients:
  - 2oz Peaty scotch **(Laphroaig 10)**
  - 0.5oz Maple syrup
  - 2 dashes Chocolate bitters
  - Orange peel
steps:
  - In a freezer chilled rocks glass, combine bitters, maple syrup, and scotch
  - Add a large ice cube and gently stir until cold
  - Express an orange peel over the top and serve
notes:
  - I find the 4:1 ratio of peaty scotch to maple syrup a bit on the sweeter side, so sometimes go up to 5:1, depending on the scotch.
  - In terms of scotch, Laphroaig 10 has been the standout winner for my own palate. I've tried some cheaper/ mixing scotch like Isle of Skye Double Smoke, but they don't have the depth of flavor needed to balance out the maple syrup.
  - I've tried it with Fee Brothers Aztec chocolate bitters, Angostura cocoa bitters, and Woodford Reserve bourbon barrel aged chocolate bitters. The chocolate bitter choice changes the flavor slightly, but all were good.
  - I've also noticed that nicer maple syrup adds an interesting complexity, although any pure maple syrup works well.
  - I tend to build it in the glass out of laziness **(I don't think it materially impacts the flavor as long as you use a freezer chilled glass)**.
  - "This to me tastes like a more refined version of the [Smokey Old Fashioned](/smokey-old-fashioned.html), since the full base spirit being scotch means you get more of the scotch nuance. It also explains why for this version I require a better scotch, whereas in the Smokey Old Fashioned the bourbon carries a lot of the flavor backbone, and the scotch is predominantly there to provide smokiness."
  - Overall I'm pleasantly surprised at how such a simple cocktail tastes so great. It really is more than the sum of its parts.
basedon:
  - "[Laphroaig Bold Fashioned](https://www.laphroaig.com/whisky-cocktails/bold-fashioned)"
---
