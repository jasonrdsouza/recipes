---
title: "Scotch Old Fashioned"
template: recipe.mustache
slug: scotch-old-fashioned
time: "2 minutes"
makes: "1 cocktail"
cocktail: |
  # Scotch Old Fashioned
  family: old-fashioned
  glass: old-fashioned
  ice: large-cube
  method: build, stir

  spine:
    2    scotch
    0.5  maple-syrup

  accent:
    2 dash chocolate-bitters

  garnish: orange-peel (expressed)
ingredients:
  - 2 oz peaty scotch **(Laphroaig 10)**
  - 0.5 oz pure maple syrup **(or ~0.4 oz, depending on the scotch)**
  - 2 dashes chocolate bitters
  - Orange peel
steps:
  - In a freezer chilled rocks glass, combine bitters, maple syrup and scotch
  - Add a large ice cube and stir gently for several turns
  - Express the orange peel over the glass
notes:
  - I find the source's 4:1 ratio of scotch to maple syrup a bit on the sweeter side, so sometimes go up to 5:1, depending on the scotch.
  - Laphroaig 10 has been the standout winner for my palate. Cheaper/ mixing scotches like Isle of Skye Double Smoke don't have the depth of flavor needed to balance out the maple syrup. I haven't tried the Sherry Oak Finish that the source calls for yet.
  - I've tried Fee Brothers Aztec chocolate bitters, Angostura cocoa bitters, and Woodford Reserve bourbon barrel aged chocolate bitters. The choice of bitters changes the flavor slightly, but all were good.
  - Any pure maple syrup works well, but a nicer maple syrup adds an interesting complexity.
  - The source stirs and strains this over fresh ice, but I tend to build it in the glass out of laziness. I don't think it materially impacts the flavor, as long as you use a freezer chilled glass.
  - "This tastes like a more refined version of the [Smokey Old Fashioned](/smokey-old-fashioned.html), since having scotch as the full base spirit means you get more of the scotch nuance. It's also why this version requires a better scotch: in the Smokey Old Fashioned the bourbon carries a lot of the flavor backbone, and the scotch is predominantly there to provide smokiness."
  - The source also garnishes with a tiny pinch of sea salt and a mist of Laphroaig from an atomiser, which I haven't experimented with yet.
  - I'm pleasantly surprised at how such a simple cocktail tastes so great. It really is more than the sum of its parts.
basedon:
  - "[Laphroaig Bold Fashioned](https://www.laphroaig.com/whisky-cocktails/bold-fashioned)"
---
